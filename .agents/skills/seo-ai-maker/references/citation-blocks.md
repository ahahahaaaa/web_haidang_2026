# Citation Blocks

## Goal

Give answer engines and human readers a compact, trustworthy summary near the top of important pages.

## Recommended shape

- 1 short summary paragraph
- 3 to 5 concrete bullets
- 2 to 4 relevant internal links
- Optional updated label when real page freshness exists

## Good content

- Direct answer to the primary query
- Scope, fit, cost, timeline, or decision context
- Specific language grounded in the actual page content

## Avoid

- vague marketing copy
- keyword stuffing
- repeating schema labels in visible text
- unsupported claims or invented numbers
